An offline guide and tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI — official names, descriptions and grades, a cited guide for every trophy, a roadmap to the platinum, missable trophies flagged, and a checklist with saved progress, filters, a spoiler setting, and export/import. Runs fully offline in the desktop browser; no uploads, no network, no Rockstar artwork — all art and text is original.

## Entry files

`main.ts` copies the static app and screenshots into `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`) and writes the make.fun dossier. Screenshots are in `screenshots/`.
